Bingyao Yu

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, supervised by Prof. Jiwen Lu. Prior to that, I obtained my Ph.D degree from Tsinghua University in 2023, advised by Prof. Jiwen Lu, Prof. Jie Zhou and Prof. Xiu Li. In 2018, I received my BS degree from the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University. My current research focuses on AI safety and Embodied Intelligence.
